# Glyphs and words

Every primitive has a glyph and a word: `⍳ 10 ▷ +/` is `iota 10 |> sum`.
Write either, or mix them.

| Type | Get | Word |
|---|---|---|
| `\i` | `⍳` | `iota` |
| `\e` | `¨` | `each` |
| `\k` | `⌿` | `keep` |

**Mimic: Insert Glyph…** lists them all; type a word or what it does.
**Mimic: Convert to Glyphs** and **Convert to Words** change a whole file.
